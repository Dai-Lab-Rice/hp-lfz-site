# Shutdown guide
**Purpose**: A reference to be used *each and every time* by experienced users of the furnace.

---

## Section I: Power down the furnace
1. Turn off emission on all lasers, and verify that the output power reads "OFF" in the Laser Controller Software.

1. Turn off the translation and rotation.

1. Stop the screen recorder.

1. Stop the pyrometer Data Recorder.

1. Allow chamber to cool to a reasonable temperature.

---
## Section II: Depressurize the chamber

1. Turn off the compressor.

1. Ensure the getter valves are both closed.

1. Close the gas bottle.

1. Use the vent valves on the low-pressure to depressurize the lines. Close the vent valves after you are done with this step.

1. Once the chamber is cool, open the chamber outlet ball valve, followed by slowly opening the chamber outlet needle valve to allow a small flow. You may have to continue opening the needle valve as the chamber depressurizes to maintain a constant flow rate. Once you reach zero pressure, open the needle valve all the way to ensure the chamber is fully vented.

1. If the inlet ball valve is closed, do not open it. This will cause a sudden burst of gas into the chamber. Rather, wait until the chamber has been completely unloaded and cleaned before opening the ball valve.

1. Otherwise, if the inlet ball valve is open, slowly crack open the inlet needle valve to release the pressure between the compressor and the chamber.

---

## Section III: Remove sample and clean furnace

1. ***Verify that BOTH gauges read zero***, and leave the outlet valves open.

1. Remove the top set of clamps and place in their designated location.

1. Gently slide the top translator housing upwards and remove the remains of the feed rod, as well as the washers and collars. Consider vacuuming any loose powder.

1. Pull straight down on the translation shaft, to decouple it from the magnet pack. It is important to pull straight down, any sideways force on the translation shaft can cause serious damage to the coupling to the magnet pack.

1. Clean the length of the upper shaft using a Kimwipe + IPA/EtOH, then push it upward to recouple to the magnet pack. Ensure that it is in the deepest energetic minimum of the magnet, which should be the second "click".

1. Translate the upper shaft upwards until it is inside the inconel adapter. Do not reach the upper limit.

1. Vacuum any loose powder in the translator.

1. Using a Kimwipe, IPA/EtOH, and some small tweezers preferably with tip that is not sharp to avoid scratches on the sealing surface, clean the inside of the top translator.

1. Remove the top seal ring and return to its designated location.

1. Remove the top shroud.

1. Clean the top shroud using a Kimwipe + IPA/EtOH. Do not sonicate it. Return the shroud to its designated container.

1. Remove bottom set of clamps.

1. Gently slide the bottom translator housing downwards.

1. Carefully remove the shroud window, clean it, and return it to its designated location or glass waste bin.

1. Translate the seed rod upwards until the set screw on the bottom of the seed rod holder is visible above the shroud. 

1. Carefully remove the crystal and the seed rod mount from the shaft. The crystal may be weakly attached to the seed rod, so it may be wise to grab the crystal first before removing the seed rod mount. Consider using aluminum foil as a safety net for the crystal.

1. Don't forget to take a picture of your crystal next to a ruler before destroying it!

1. Remove the bottom seal ring and return to its designated location.

1. Remove the bottom shroud.

1. Clean the bottom shroud using a Kimwipe + IPA/EtOH. Do not sonicate it. Return the shroud to its designated container.

1. Consider vacuuming or collecting any loose powder.

1. Remove washer, collar, and shroud cup. Be careful, as these may be full with powder after a volatile growth.

1. Vacuum any loose powder in the translator.

1. Pull straight up on the translation shaft, to decouple it from the magnet pack. It is important to pull straight up, any sideways force on the translation shaft can cause serious damage to the coupling to the magnet pack.

1. Clean the length of the upper shaft, then push it upward to recouple to the magnet pack. Ensure that it is in the deepest energetic minimum of the magnet, which should be the second "click".

1. Using a Kimwipe, IPA/EtOH, and some small tweezers, clean the inside of the translator.

1. Using a ball of Kimwipes, IPA/EtOH, and the long tweezers (forceps), clean the inside of the chamber.

1. Clean the interior and exterior off the shroud pieces and shroud window.

1. Place all small hardware in a beaker with some IPA/EtOH, and use the sonicator to clean these.

1. Retract both the top and bottom translator shafts, and slide both translators towards the chamber before leaving.
